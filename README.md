

# Karen Adkins Snider

### AI Evaluator · Writer · Editor

I’m a former newspaper reporter who moved into AI evaluation, combining an editorial background with more than four years of experience working with AI-generated content.

My work focuses on evaluating whether model outputs are accurate, useful, well-written, and faithful to instructions. I’m particularly interested in the places where language, reasoning, and model behavior collide.

## 🔎 Areas of Focus

- AI response evaluation and quality assurance
- Instruction-following and constraint adherence
- Editorial review and content quality
- Factuality and hallucination detection
- Ambiguity and plausibility analysis
- Prompt design and testing
- Rubric-based evaluation
- AI safety and model behavior

## 🧰 Background

Before working with AI, I spent eight years in newspaper journalism, where I developed a healthy suspicion of unsupported claims, vague language, and sentences that should have been put out of their misery three clauses ago.

I've since applied those instincts to evaluating AI systems and generated content across a variety of tasks and domains.
